The bar at home in three steps

ice it, spike it, jimmy it

1

Ice it

fill the glass
Start with a glass full of ice. Jimmy's tastes best cold, and the ice does half the work.

2

Spike it

60 ml of spirit
Add 60 ml of your favourite spirit. Gin, vodka, rum, whiskey or tequila, the mixer is made for all of them.

3

Jimmy it

pour & stir
Pour in Jimmy's, stir and serve. For a perfect serve, shake it in a cocktail shaker and pour over ice.

0

Mocktail

skip the spirit
Enjoy it straight up over ice. Same flavour, no alcohol.
