frequently asked questions

Is it Alcoholic Or Non Alcoholic?
Jimmy’s Drinks are premium non-alcoholic cocktail mixers.
So how do I make it a cocktail?
Just pour Jimmy’s and 60 ml of your favorite spirit over ice, stir & serve. Your favorite drink is ready!

For a perfect serve, mix above in a cocktail shaker & serve over ice.
Can kids have this instead of juices and soft drinks?
Absolutely, we use only high-quality ingredients with lower sugar content that both kids and adults can enjoy, as a mocktail!
How many drinks can I make from one bottle of Jimmy’s?
Each Jimmy’s bottle is 250 ml, and makes 2 drinks. That is assuming a standard whisky glass mixed with 60 ml spirit.
