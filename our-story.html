the rest of the story
Jimmy's Cocktails are made to mix perfectly with your favourite spirit or enjoy straight up as a Mocktail. Crafted by some of India's top mixologists, our mixers are made with fewer calories and zero artificial sweeteners: achieved by using only the highest quality ingredients with their natural sweetness. Jimmy's Cocktails deliver all the complexity and balance of flavors you would find at your local bar.
Jimmy's is made by Radiohead Brands, a Gurugram-based beverage start-up. Created to bring the bar experience home, Jimmy's debuted with four non-alcoholic mixers in 2019 and added two more in 2021, crafted at the Jimmy's Cocktail Labs with Yangdup Lama, one of the world's leading mixologists.
In 2022 the company raised INR 14 crore in a Pre Series-A round led by Roots Ventures, launched a range of sparkling beverages in cans, and collaborated with Greater Than Gin, Sleepy Owl Coffee and Dewar's on signature mixers. In 2023 Radiohead Brands became a multi-beverage company with the launch of Hustle Energy Drink, and Jimmy's non-alcoholic beer followed.
